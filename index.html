About ResoRights AI

What ResoRights AI is — and isn't

ResoRights AI is a legal information resource, not a law firm. It provides general information about California law and does not constitute legal advice, create an attorney-client relationship, or substitute for the advice of a licensed attorney.

If your situation involves active litigation, is time-sensitive, or you are unsure about your rights, we encourage you to contact a licensed California attorney or a legal aid organization in your area.
